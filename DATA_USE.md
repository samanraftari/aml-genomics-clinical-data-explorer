# Data use and privacy

This project uses open-access, de-identified TCGA-LAML PanCancer Atlas data obtained through cBioPortal.

## Public repository policy

- Do not commit raw clinical, sample, mutation, germline, BAM, FASTQ, or other patient-level source files.
- Do not attempt re-identification or linkage to identifiable sources.
- Publish code, documentation, aggregate tables, and non-identifying figures only.
- Stop if a source requires a dbGaP token, institutional authorization, or controlled-access agreement.
- Preserve source, study identifier, access date, licence, and publication citations.

## Scope

This is an educational portfolio analysis, not human-subject recruitment, clinical research, or a clinical decision-support system. Findings must not be used for diagnosis, prognosis, or treatment decisions.
